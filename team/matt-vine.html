Matt Vine

Senior Associate

Matt is a Senior Associate on RRE's investment team, focusing on early-stage investing.

Matt is passionate about partnering with bold, curious founders who are leveraging emerging technologies in exciting, disruptive ways. Matt joined RRE after earning his MBA from Columbia Business School, where he also worked for Dorm Room Fund, the premier student venture fund backing student-led startups. Prior to business school, Matt worked at an early-stage venture firm, and before that, held several roles at Citibank, including serving as the Quantum Computing Lead for the Innovation Labs, working on the Emerging Technologies team at Citi Ventures, and contributing to the hyperconverged infrastructure engineering team within Citi Technology Infrastructure. Each role offered the chance to collaborate with exceptional engineers and builders to advance Citi’s technology capabilities. Outside of work, Matt is an avid reader (especially of Neal Stephenson and sci-fi), anime enthusiast, and huge fan of Vince Gilligan’s TV shows.

Matt holds an MBA from Columbia Business School and has dual degrees in Integrated Business and Engineering and Industrial Systems Engineering from Lehigh University. He also serves on the Alumni Board for Hopkins School in New Haven Connecticut.
